Pop It is a rhythm game on a glowing fidget board. Cue lights flash to the beat — tap the right stone at the right time.

PLAY
• Rhythm modes: Classic, Survival, Precision, and Endless Rush
• Difficulty from Easy to Expert
• Daily challenge — one shared run per day
• Versus bots (2–4 players) locally
• Road to Glory survival climb

PROGRESS
• Grades, personal bests, and combo chains
• Unlockable visual themes
• Local day streak
